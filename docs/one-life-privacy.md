# Sparky privacy notice

## What stays on your device

Sparky has no Sparky user account, marketing list, or publisher dashboard for reading your in-app activity. Habit logs, goals, focus history, journals, check-ins and settings are stored on your device. Optional permissions let the app read selected Health Connect, app usage and calendar data for the corresponding features. Android handles notifications, alarms and Do Not Disturb according to your choices. Dictation uses the speech service selected on your device, which may process audio under its own privacy policy.

## Optional connections and sharing

Connecting ChatGPT uses OpenAI sign-in and authorization for your account. When you send a question, OpenAI receives the text and up to six previous question and answer pairs from the active conversation. You can separately choose to include the app context shown in the preview. A manual AI report sends the report prompt shown before sending. Program tips send the program title, the current exercise and the question you enter. Weekly AI reviews start off. If you enable them after reviewing the disclosure, the app can send aggregate habit counts to OpenAI while the app is closed. Weekly prompts omit habit names, notes, device health imports and screen time. Turn off weekly reviews in Coach or sign out to stop scheduling them. OpenAI handles what it receives under its applicable terms and privacy policy.

Sparky stores up to 100 chats on the device and 50 question and answer pairs per chat. Coach uses an identifier derived from your verified ChatGPT account to open, continue, delete or export that account's chats. Signing out clears the open conversation, draft and context selection. Saved chats remain on the device. The sign-in chooser can show the email addresses of previously connected accounts. Older chats without an account identifier are available through a full device backup.

Export chat saves only the selected conversation as readable JSON, including its questions, answers and timestamps. An answer may repeat sensitive details from a shared prompt. The file remains at the chosen destination after sign-out, reset or uninstall.

Connecting Google Tasks lets the app use Google authorization to read your open tasks. Google handles authorization and requests under its privacy policy. Tasks are not required to use Sparky. If you select an app in Android's sharing menu or choose a backup destination, that recipient or storage provider receives the items you choose to send. Select private destinations for sensitive data.

## Backups, recovery and retention

Settings lets anyone with access to Sparky export a full device backup, even while signed out of ChatGPT. Manual and automatic backups can include every account's saved chats and account identifiers, older chats, journals, reflections, saved AI reviews, imported health data and app usage. Anyone with the file can read it. ChatGPT sign-in tokens are stored separately. Choose a private destination. Larger copies use the Sparky JSON archive format. Sparky can also restore older JSON backups. Large restores require enough free memory and local storage. Import shows a preview before replacing local records. Keep the original file until you verify your restored history.

Automatic copies are off by default. On Android 10 or later, you can allow them in Settings. While the app is open it checks for changes every minute and retries failed writes. It keeps the latest verified copy for each of up to 30 days in Downloads/Sparky. Settings shows the last verified copy time. The browser and Android 9 support manual export. Changes since the last copy may need to be entered again after recovery.

Reset clears saved chats and records, disables automatic copies and weekly AI sharing, and cancels scheduled weekly reviews. You can also delete automatic copies owned by this installation during reset or later in Settings. Earlier versions may have created copies before this choice was available. After reset or uninstall, check Downloads/Sparky in Files and remove remaining copies yourself. Also remove manual exports from their chosen destinations and empty trash where applicable.

## Permissions, storage and deletion

Change optional permissions in Android Settings. Pause or clear imported history in Sparky. Sign out of ChatGPT in the coach. Hiding Google Tasks closes the list. Disconnecting clears the access token on this device. To revoke Sparky's authorization at Google, remove its access in Google Account connections. Use Settings to export or reset your local records, or clear Sparky's storage in Android Settings. Delete manual exports separately. Resetting Sparky does not erase data already sent to a service or copies controlled by a recipient. Use that service's privacy controls for those copies.

## Contact and privacy rights

The publisher does not operate a server for your habit records. If you contact support, the publisher receives the address and information you choose to send and uses it to respond. Where applicable law gives you access, correction, deletion or other rights over data the publisher holds, use the support email in the Google Play listing to exercise them. You may also contact your local data protection authority. OpenAI and Google publish their own privacy policies.
